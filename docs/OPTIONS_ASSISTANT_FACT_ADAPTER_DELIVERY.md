# Bounded assistant research facts — October 12, 2026

The existing simulated-account spread preparation can now represent assistant
research honestly while retaining the source originals that support ordinary
fundamental/news facts. A missing exact publication second is no longer replaced
with a fabricated timestamp or a provider/Owner authorship label. This delivery
does not make the saved R2 candidate tradable: its actual quote conditions failed,
and original contract, risk and data-evidence gaps remain.

- `TECH_SPREAD_PLAN_INPUT_V3` explicitly selects
  `SIMULATED_ASSISTANT_FACTS_V1`, only in `REAL_QUOTE_PAPER` and the existing
  simulated account context. Real-source scope is the single existing
  `paper-sim-tech-1000-20261010` account; isolated fixtures have their own aliases.
- Research uses `THREE_FACET_INPUT_V2` / `ASSISTANT_RESEARCH`. The request uses
  `SYSTEM_PAPER_REQUEST`; neither is attributed to a provider or the Owner.
  Only AAPL/TSM equities, one CDS/PDS spread, and the existing $1,000 account
  ceiling are supported. This ceiling is not a new global risk setting.
- The saved plan carries `TECH_SPREAD_PLAN_BINDING_V2` and the policy marker;
  restore validates the same sources, context, account, clocks and original
  evidence gates. Legacy input/binding versions retain their existing route.
- All eight evidence kinds remain required. No contract, product, funds, costs,
  entitlement, liquidity, risk, source freshness, spread, sequence or selection
  requirement is waived. This adds no automatic admission, collection or order.

## Source facts and timing

Each fact references a retained original tool-receipt JSON string and SHA-256,
an HTTPS source URL, and an exact excerpt from the same titled source block.
The retained receipt has distinct request/return clocks. The extraction is
explicitly assistant-authored and the fact remains a publisher claim. Hashes
and matching excerpts verify local bytes/attribution, not publisher authenticity,
semantic truth or independent corroboration. The directional thesis remains a
separate assistant hypothesis.

Bounds are four originals, twelve facts, 65,536 characters per original, 60,000
characters per tool result, 1,200 characters per excerpt, and 262,144 characters
for the new research envelope. Old envelope limits remain unchanged.

Ordinary facts retain unknown, calendar-date, UTC-minute or interval publication
precision and `publishedAt: null`. Their conservative availability boundary must
equal the actual original receipt and precede registration/review/assessment;
nanosecond comparisons are retained. A partial minute/interval extending beyond
the receipt is withheld. These publication descriptions are attributed
extractions, not authenticated provider clocks. Unknown availability stays blocked.

The new adapter **does not admit exact-event facts or exact publication claims**.
An observed dated news trigger also stays blocked on this route. Independent
review found that a declared exact time could otherwise be invented without
changing the original; the final implementation rejects that path. Exact timed
events still require the separate existing exact-evidence route.
