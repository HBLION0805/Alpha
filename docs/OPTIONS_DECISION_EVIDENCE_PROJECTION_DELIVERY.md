# Options Decision Evidence Projection V1 delivery

Decision Evidence is an additive, immutable local projection after successful Fast Host FINISH. It reads the exact verified issued guidance report and its saved input, the bound Prediction Evidence artifact, capture, and attributed analyst note. It feeds those pinned values into the existing daily decision card helper and assesses capital policy from the issued input settings. Later workbench state, settings, and clocks cannot change an artifact under the same identity. No historical Decision records are backfilled.

Each artifact has GLD and IBIT only. Each candidate records `NO_TRADE` because Prediction Evidence V1 lacks a forecast horizon, frozen plan, and Owner authority. BULLISH/BEARISH analysis supplies UP/DOWN conditional direction; MIXED and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE supply null. Original issued disposition, full blockers, conditional entry text, attributed invalidation, eventFacts snapshot, source clocks, illustrative reference contracts and capital policy remain auditable. Entry trigger, numeric entry/stop/target, exact time exit, approved risk, frozen plan, confidence, fill and win probability remain null. References are not chosen trades or approved net targets. All artifact and candidate authority flags deny execution and canonical decision/prediction eligibility.

The ignored local store is `data/runtime/options-decision-evidence/`. Its report/Prediction Evidence key is deterministic. Bounded safe reads, canonical bytes, integrity hash and exclusive writes reject corruption, mismatched identities, unsafe paths and symlinks. `--decision-evidence` and `/api/state.decisionEvidence` read only; absent predecessor evidence remains `NOT_MATERIALIZED` with a dependency reason. Operationally failed FINISH reports `SKIPPED` and creates no Decision artifact. A projection error fails FINISH closed. The pure validator and saved-artifact verifier are available for an independent verifier.

## Alpha1550Verifier V3 reference

For a saved successful FINISH receipt and current Decision view, require matching Decision identity/path/count and disabled execution; require `MATERIALIZED` or the equivalent verified existing status. Load the exact bound Prediction Evidence and invoke the independent `verifyDecisionEvidenceArtifact(root, predictionEvidence)` and `validateDecisionEvidence(decision, predictionEvidence)` checks. Compare report, capture, analysis and Prediction Evidence bindings to the receipt, plus the Decision view identity. Require exactly GLD/IBIT; all artifact and candidate authority flags false except `ownerAuthorityRequired=true`; `NO_TRADE`, null calibration and exits, explicit blockers and unknowns. An operational FAIL must have `SKIPPED` and no Decision artifact for that finish identity. Do not call market, model, account, position, order or source tools; do not touch the installed verifier. A current view after a newer capture may target a different identity, so a mismatch is a failed acceptance, not grounds to rewrite an earlier artifact.
