## What each source supports

The three saved provider metadata reads establish the two selected instrument
UUIDs, shared SPY chain, October 16 expiry, call type and 778/779 strikes. Reported
active/tradable state, monetary multiplier and tick increments remain historical
source fields. They do not establish broker permission or current tradability.

The saved Cboe SPY-specific reference supports American exercise as a mapped
product rule. Its 100-SPY-share physical-delivery example supports the standard
product convention. Applying that convention to these two exact series remains
conditional. The saved schema defines chain_symbol as an underlying ticker and
the price multiplier as a monetary conversion, not a share count. No cash
component cannot establish the stock quantity. Current standard deliverable and
adjustment applicability therefore remain unresolved.

An acceptable future evidence chain can directly associate either exact UUID or
a reliable complete series identity with its current deliverable/adjustment
description. Alternatively, a documented current standard-series classification
and traceable exact identity mapping can support the product-rule mapping. Keep
source text and locator, version, applicable date, mapping steps and conflicts.
No bespoke per-contract certificate or proof of never having been adjusted is
required. A Boolean assertion, missing adjustment notice, multiplier or ordinary
SPY ticker does not establish applicability. This page neither creates such an
envelope nor qualifies a contract; existing permission, entitlement, costs, risk
and prospective quote requirements remain separate.
