# Weekly trading plan V1

The weekly plan is a local preparation record, separate from Daily Guidance and
individual trade authorization. It covers the Monday–Friday period containing the
current New York weekday; on a weekend it targets the following Monday–Friday.
Weekday selection does not assert that a market is open. Reading or previewing a
plan makes no market, account, order, public-source or model request.

The preview composes previously saved, validated BLS and FOMC calendar evidence.
An exact BLS release time remains an exact time; a FOMC meeting known only by date
remains date-only. The record retains source references and the saved retrieval
clock. Missing source evidence means unknown coverage, never an event-free week.
Cancelled source events remain visible as cancellation evidence, but do not enter
the active daily checkpoints; tentative events remain explicitly tentative.
Scheduled events and unscheduled news-risk watch topics are separate lists.
Focused News, Macro Context, Event Research, Shopping List and previously saved
guidance may supply context or links, but do not become calendar authority or
trading direction. The Shopping List is a research universe, including its
identity and status caveats, not a ranked buy list.

Supported categories are `ECONOMIC_DATA`, `CENTRAL_BANK`, `EARNINGS`,
`COMPANY_EVENT`, `CONFERENCE`, `TREASURY_OR_FISCAL`,
`POLICY_GEOPOLITICAL`, `OPTIONS_MARKET`, `OTHER_SCHEDULED_EVENT` and
`UNSCHEDULED_NEWS_WATCH`. Timing precision is `EXACT_TIME`, `DATE_ONLY`,
`DATE_RANGE` or `UNKNOWN`; an unknown intraday time is never made midnight.
Nine independent coverage domains report `REVIEWED`, `MISSING_SOURCE`,
`UNKNOWN` or `NOT_APPLICABLE`. The current saved source set has no complete
earnings or conference calendar, so neither domain can be called complete.
Manually supplied events retain `OWNER_DECLARED` or `UNVERIFIED` provenance even
when a citation is supplied. Their source ID must be nonempty and their status
cannot claim source confirmation. An unscheduled news watch is not a manual
scheduled event.

The Owner can preview a draft, explicitly review it, and later append an
amendment. Each saved revision is immutable, fingerprinted, ordered, and linked
to the preceding version. A stale expected fingerprint fails instead of
overwriting another revision. A review preserves the information then available;
later information cannot be written into the earlier snapshot. There is no
automatic weekly plan approval or scheduled background work. A missing or
incomplete weekly plan is visible on 总览 and 每日决策, but is **not** a new Daily
Guidance blocker. A plan or event cannot produce a trade, order, prediction,
fill, or capital allocation.
The review endpoint accepts only the active New York target week, preventing a
later request from posing as a prior prospective review. The page displays the
saved revision as its original snapshot; newly recomposed source evidence is
separately labelled as an unsaved preview until an amendment is explicitly saved.

The first read-only preview covers 2026-09-28 through 2026-10-02 from saved local
sources. It may remain an incomplete draft. Its actual event list and receipt
references belong in the delivery record after the real local read. Review and
amendment must use the protected local Workbench session; preview alone writes no
plan revision.
