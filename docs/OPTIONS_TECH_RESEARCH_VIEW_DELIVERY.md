# Saved three-facet research in the existing workbench

October 10, 2026. Development branch only; no deployment or source acquisition.

The existing Event research page's Technology research and Paper section previously showed workflow receipts, blockers and Paper status, but omitted saved three-facet reasoning and conditions. It now includes expandable research versions in that same section. No parallel page or new recommendation model was added.

The published `OptionsThreeFacet` contract, engine and fixtures already existed at base commit `64d2145`. This increment uses that engine at each saved input's original `asOf` clock and the published `readTechEventReviews` verified replay. The old uncommitted three-facet CLI, POST route, package scripts, standalone tests and historical delivery documents are excluded. The isolated release still returns 404 for `POST /api/three-facet`.

## What is displayed

- Separate fundamental price direction and operating trend; news timing; descriptive technical structure.
- Original research assessment time, source cutoff, declared holding window, and separate publication/receipt/review clocks. Missing times remain unknown.
- Saved hypothesis, trial, confirmation, invalidation, independent exit and next-evidence summaries. Absent conditions remain unknown; they are not filled from prose.
- Necessary summaries from named fields, permitted public source links, evidence gaps and independent saved-version fingerprints. Summaries are capped at 420 characters with a visible ellipsis.
- Distinct saved research, historical attributed samples and synthetic TEST_FIXTURE contexts. The page does not label saved research as fresh market data.

The first local read found 36 records across independent saved versions: 26 saved research records, five synthetic event-review records, and five historical three-facet samples. This is not 36 independent opportunities. The latest real nine-review chain still has three qualified and 2,931 unresolved company/event associations. The page does not append reviews, merge versions, clear pending coverage or change rankings.

No technical prices, Fib levels, position sizes, probability or fills are generated by the projection. Historical inputs can retain a conditional direction despite missing technical evidence. Current quote admission is always `NOT_EVALUATED`. Original engine research states are references at their original time, not current trading permission or a guarantee of returns.

## Reading and privacy boundary

The existing loopback-only GET status/state routes carry the new projection. There is no new route, query path, background service or source request. The inventory recognizes the existing versioned technology-research receipts and the bounded `three-facet-YYYYMMDD/five-real-*/five-real-results.json` historical format. Other old research snapshot formats are not automatically inferred or imported.

The reader permits at most eight verified research runs, 800 files in a run's preflight, depth eight, 12 MiB per file and 128 MiB total input. Links, hardlinked files, changed files and invalid/future envelope clocks are refused. Existing receipt manifests and event-review chains are verified by the existing reader. Historical sample hashes identify read bytes; they do not independently authenticate the attributed sources. Unsupported/malformed materials produce a visible partial-read notice without modifying originals.

Only explicitly projected fields leave the server. Full original bodies, extracted document blocks, raw input/envelopes, local paths, account material, risk amounts and arbitrary additional fields are omitted. Summary fields containing recognizable private paths, credentials, account markers or email addresses are withheld. Public links require HTTPS and an explicit reviewed host allowlist; credentials, ports, query strings, fragments and unlisted hosts are refused. At most 16 distinct permitted sources are shown per record, with omitted counts. Links open only on user action and do not fetch on page load.
