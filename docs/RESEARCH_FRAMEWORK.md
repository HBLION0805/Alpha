# Alpha Research Framework

Version: 1.0
Revision date: 2026-07-16

## 1. Purpose

This framework defines a consistent, evidence-based, and reviewable research standard for Alpha capital decisions.

Alpha does not optimize for prediction accuracy alone. Alpha optimizes for profitable execution under controlled risk.

## 2. Research Scope

Alpha research may cover:

- Market environment
- Macro conditions
- Sector behavior
- Individual stocks
- ETFs
- Leveraged ETFs
- Inverse ETFs
- Event-driven opportunities
- Future asset classes

## 3. Research Identification

Every research project must include:

- Research ID
- Title
- Date
- Research owner
- Objective
- Time horizon
- Instruments covered
- Data timestamp
- Research status

## 4. Research Objective

Each research project must define:

- The exact question being answered
- The decision the research is intended to support
- Why the research is needed now
- What is explicitly outside scope

## 5. Evidence Standard

Research must separate:

- Verified facts
- Calculated metrics
- Inferences
- Assumptions
- Unknowns

Important claims must include:

- Source
- Timestamp
- Data period
- Confidence level

If evidence is insufficient, the report must say so.

Do not invent precision.

Do not present unsupported scores.

## 6. Market Analysis

Market analysis should include:

- Broad market condition
- Risk-on or risk-off state
- Major index behavior
- Volatility environment
- Interest-rate environment
- Market breadth
- Liquidity conditions
- Relevant macro events

## 7. Sector Analysis

Sector analysis should include:

- Sector trend
- Relative strength versus the broad market
- Sector-wide catalysts
- Sector-wide risks
- Capital rotation
- Leading and lagging companies

## 8. Company Analysis

For each company, include:

- Business quality
- Fundamental condition
- Valuation context
- Price trend
- Relative strength
- Volatility
- Liquidity
- Catalysts
- Risks
- Downside scenario
- Rebound scenario
- Trend persistence potential

## 9. Instrument Comparison

Alpha must compare available execution instruments, not only the underlying stock.

Possible instruments include:

- Common stock
- Fractional shares
- Sector ETF
- Leveraged long ETF
- Inverse ETF
- Leveraged inverse ETF
- No trade

For each instrument, compare:

- Expected return
- Expected loss
- Volatility
- Liquidity
- Spread
- Leverage
- Daily reset risk
- Holding-period suitability
- Execution risk
- Risk-adjusted expected value

The instrument with the highest possible return is not automatically the best instrument.

Alpha should select the instrument with the strongest risk-adjusted expected value.

## 10. Scenario Analysis

Every report should include:

- Bull case
- Base case
- Bear case

For each case, include:

- Probability
- Expected move
- Expected duration
- Supporting evidence
- Failure conditions

## 11. Confidence Framework

Alpha separates:

- Research Confidence
- Prediction Confidence
- Execution Confidence

Research Confidence measures evidence quality.

Prediction Confidence measures confidence in the expected direction or scenario.

Execution Confidence measures confidence that the selected instrument and plan can convert the thesis into profit under controlled risk.

## 12. Trading Plan

Every actionable recommendation must include:

- Direction
- Instrument
- Entry condition
- Entry range
- Position size
- Scaling plan
- Stop loss
- Take profit
- Partial profit-taking rules
- Maximum holding period
- Invalidation condition
- Exit condition
- Confidence
- Expected reward
- Expected risk
- Reward-to-risk ratio

A trading plan must be created before execution.

After entry, the plan is frozen except for predefined exceptional events.

## 13. Decision Outcomes

Allowed outcomes include:

- STRONG BUY
- BUY
- SMALL POSITION
- HOLD
- WAIT
- REDUCE
- EXIT
- SHORT
- NO TRADE

WAIT is a valid decision.

Cash is a valid position.

Alpha does not maximize trading frequency.

Alpha maximizes expected value.

## 14. Risk Rules

- No recommendation without a stop loss
- No recommendation without a take-profit plan
- No recommendation without an invalidation condition
- Position sizing must reflect volatility
- Leveraged and inverse ETFs require shorter holding periods and stricter controls
- Total strategy risk and individual trade risk must both be limited
- Do not average down unless the plan explicitly allows it
- Do not increase risk after entry because of emotion

## 15. Post-Trade Review

After a trade closes, record:

- Planned thesis
- Actual entry
- Actual exit
- Profit or loss
- Maximum favorable excursion
- Maximum adverse excursion
- Whether the prediction was correct
- Whether the execution was profitable
- Whether the plan was followed
- Lessons learned
- Strategy changes for future versions

Prediction accuracy and trading profitability must be evaluated separately.

## 16. Required Research Report Structure

Use this reusable report outline:

- Research ID
- Executive Summary
- Objective
- Data and Sources
- Market Overview
- Macro Analysis
- Sector Analysis
- Company Analysis
- Instrument Comparison
- Bull / Base / Bear Cases
- Risk Assessment
- Trading Plan
- Decision
- Confidence
- Unknowns
- Post-Trade Review Placeholder
- Recommended Next Step

## 17. Research Completion Report

After completing research work, report:

- Files changed
- Research completed
- Sources used
- Data timestamps
- Calculations performed
- Assumptions
- Unknowns
- Limitations
- Recommended next step
