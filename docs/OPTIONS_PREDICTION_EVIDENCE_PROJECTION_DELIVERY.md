# Options Prediction Evidence Projection V1

The successful fast-host FINISH now saves one immutable, local prediction evidence artifact after its bound guidance publish. The artifact contains exactly one GLD and one IBIT candidate. It pins the published report, capture and attributed analyst note paths and fingerprints, their clocks, and the eventFacts V1 factual rows available at the report assessment time. The workbench `/api/state` exposes `predictionEvidence`; `options-daily-guidance --prediction-evidence` reads it without materializing anything.

The host bias remains attributed analysis. Only BULLISH and BEARISH yield UP and DOWN direction candidates. MIXED and INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE yield no direction. Event surprise signs and unresolved eventFacts issues remain factual context and do not choose direction. Confidence and forecast horizon are unavailable. Every candidate blocks canonical submission on the missing horizon, frozen plan and Owner authority; no Prediction Log record is created, submitted or locked. Execution is disabled.

Artifacts live under ignored `data/runtime/options-prediction-evidence/` and are keyed to the exact report fingerprint. Existing artifacts are verified and returned idempotently. Unsafe references, corrupted records, stale capture or analysis, and mismatched published inputs fail closed. The stage reads saved local evidence only and makes no market, public source, external model, account, position or order calls. This development delivery makes no prospective real-market prediction claim for the already-completed September 29, 2026 run; natural-run acceptance remains separate.
