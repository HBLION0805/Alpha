# Fixed-pool technology candidate handoff

The existing fixed 36-company scan can now hand its highest-ranked qualified
event to saved research, bounded option-source review and explicit Paper
admission checks. `options:tech-candidate` verifies the original policy, source
responses, company coverage, retrospective rules, ranking and later review.
It copies a self-contained private bundle before quote review; assessments append.

The local real-source run replayed the original 36-company ranking and 80 source
response hashes, selecting TSLA ahead of SNPS under the existing rules. This was
not a new scan or a five-company manual substitute. Event priority does not
establish price direction, valuation, consensus surprise or company-wide coverage.
Excluding one event never excludes every opportunity in that company.

TSLA is the only new symbol added to the existing quote and feasibility adapters.
The one-shot source path returned two exact contracts during a closed session.
Their original source clocks were historical, before the new research. Acquisition,
mapping, persistence and linked rejection were exercised; live Paper admission
and an entry/exit lifecycle were not. No retries or new collector were introduced.

Research can continue while data entitlement, product permission, funds and risk
limits remain individually UNKNOWN. Those gaps block admission without deciding
company direction. Costs, deliverable, technical confirmation and hedge comparison
remain separate gaps. The inherited spread formula is an engineering reference,
not an Owner rule; its technology applicability remains unapproved and unchanged.

Private scan responses, quote IDs/prices, local paths and receipts remain ignored.
No account access, order, challenge-ledger write, production schedule, CPI record,
deployment or main-branch merge is part of this delivery.
