# Saved engineering acceptance in the read-only workbench

The Owner requested an option engineering follow-on using only the existing
account's settled cash, and a website view of the actual stock and option results.
The bounded exact-series preflight stopped before a quote or entry because the
returned contract metadata did not establish deliverable components/share quantity,
exercise style or adjustment status. A price multiplier is not a share count.
General product conventions and chain availability do not establish those exact
series facts or broker account permission. No alternative legs or retries followed.

This change adds only the saved-result reader and an existing-layout panel. It does
not add an option execution engine or create an account continuation. The retained
proposal would use an exclusive, parent-tip-bound segment inside the same account,
the same writer lock and the original closed-stock cash state; it would never reset
the original start, deposit money or spend unsettled sale proceeds. Its cash debit
ceiling is separate from the unchanged debit-less-than-spread-width check.

## Verification and display

An explicit ignored `engineering-acceptance.json` binds one stock run, one blocked
option preflight and the original three account files by hash. The reader verifies
the complete stock source bundles through pure journal replay, compares the saved
report and fresh-process recovery receipt, and retains the recorded independent
review. The option view checks the three metadata manifests, transcript/event and
request/response bindings, exact selected series and missing-field conclusion.
Hashes establish local integrity and lineage, not provider authentication.

Only bounded display fields leave the reader: simulation balances, prices, model
P&L, run/commit references, source/request/application clocks and the exact evidence
gaps. Original responses, Host logs, credentials and local paths are not serialized.
The original technology data root remains separate from the explicit engineering
data root. No lookup joins unrelated research by symbol.

The new card distinguishes stock engineering acceptance from an unstarted option
lifecycle, and settled cash from sale proceeds that remain unsettled. Research and
strategy are not applicable to this engineering test. No option lifecycle or
two-week strategy validation is claimed. Old NO_TRADE records remain unchanged.

Any changed account file, additional event/continuation, source mismatch or future
binding stops display of old balances and success. The reader never invokes
recovery, takes a lock, writes a journal or calls a source. Existing read-only
HTTP/process protections and all eleven navigation routes remain required.

## Delivery boundary

The local 4178 update requires a verified pinned archive and independent browser
acceptance before switching. Retain the previous reviewed archive for rollback.
Real captures and account records stay ignored/local. Public push requires its
own exact-scope approval. No main merge, public deployment, real order, new source
schedule, CPI/15:50 or challenge-ledger change is included.

Validation and actual-port acceptance are recorded separately in
[the status file](status/engineering-acceptance-web.json). The original stock
delivery checkpoint remains its historical pre-execution checkpoint; actual
outcomes are read from private originals rather than rewritten into that record.
