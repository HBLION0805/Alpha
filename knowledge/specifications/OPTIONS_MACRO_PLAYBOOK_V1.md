# Macro playbook V1

Owner request: incorporate the supplied macro trading education, stop/exit experiences
and conceptual profit curves into Alpha, ahead of a later psychology upload.

## Bounded delivery

An English, source-attributed reference catalog and a local decision worksheet in
the workbench. The catalog distinguishes process guidance, checked mechanics,
untested personal thresholds and illustrations. Each item states its application
to GLD/IBIT, limitations and the existing workflow destination. It does not become
approved strategy knowledge or change canonical daily guidance.

The linked site is a sixteen-chapter course. Review coverage is the full syllabus
and chapters 11 and 16; remaining chapter bodies are not represented as reviewed.
User-supplied prose/graphs are experience, not observed market data. Selected
mechanical claims are checked against OIC and FINRA; no claim of a general edge.

## Decision worksheet

Record ETF, title, PRE_TRADE_NOTE or REVIEW_NOTE, reference to an original plan,
macro and holding horizons, source clocks/expectations, transmission and alternate
scenarios, ETF confirmation, contract/IV fit, combined exposure, separate thesis,
price and time exits, overnight/monitoring contingencies, costs and integer exits.
Missing answers remain explicitly undocumented. Nonempty prose is only a coverage
check; software cannot qualify its truth or trading merit.

Self-reported process adherence and self-reported financial outcome are separate.
Neither proves skill, luck, cause, actual fills or audited PnL. Pre-trade notes have
no outcome assessment. Review classifications require an original-plan reference
and an evidence explanation; missing support leaves the process unknown. Saved
notes have server receipt clocks; PRE_TRADE_NOTE is a user's label, not proof it
preceded an entry. No backfill into the frozen prospective trend study.

Explicit save writes an exclusive, append-only, fingerprinted record to ignored
runtime storage. Copy the complete catalog and assessed input into each record;
recompute against its copied catalog on read. Stable request IDs permit an exact
retry but reject changed content. Revisions use new records and reference old IDs.
Corrupt records remain visible as errors without hiding valid records. Do not
expose private attachment paths or fingerprints in the public frontend catalog.

## Content review requirements

- Preserve horizon distinctions, source clocks, expectations vs actuals, alternate
  explanations and independent outcome evidence.
- Author's -7%, 30/30, 30–50%, 80–100%, 90/10, 50/25/25 and one-loss-add rules are
  inactive candidates. No arbitrary default is installed in trading engines.
- Defined risk at expiration is not a guaranteed stop or frictionless exercise;
  describe leg/assignment/expiration risk. Long options already limit premium loss.
- Stop trigger is not a guaranteed fill; stop-limit can remain unfilled.
- Use whole contracts; do not round fractions or increase size to implement runners.
  Recovering gross premium is not fee-inclusive recovery; a runner has market value.
- Illustrated PnL paths have no predictive classification; shared prefixes can end
  differently. No inferred dealer positioning, stop manipulation or psychology.
- A thesis remaining plausible does not cancel pre-existing price/time/exposure
  limits. A failed thesis cannot be extended by an untested 30/30 grace period.
- Reading, travel and rest are personal process aids, not validated return factors.
  Calendar stereotypes and leaving screens do not establish safe monitoring.
