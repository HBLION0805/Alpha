# Macro political and narrative discipline V3

This September 24, 2026 Owner-supplied batch extends the existing Macro
playbook as education and optional reflection. Its content revision is
`OWNER_PSYCHOLOGY_20260924_V3`; the note contract remains
`OPTIONS_MACRO_PLAYBOOK_V1`. V1/V2 notes retain their original copied catalogs
and bytes. There is no political model, scoring system or trading rule.

`psych-political-entity-separation` is one new `PROCESS_GUIDE`: identify the
speaker and authority, then distinguish country, government, party, population,
statement, proposal, enacted policy, implementation, economic consequence and
market reaction. Apply the same evidence standard to favored and disfavored
actors. Record observed facts, attributed statements, interpretations,
assumptions and unknowns separately; trace the economic channel and contrary
evidence instead of averaging political narratives.

Existing `psych-news`, `psych-second-order` and `psych-short-weakness` add
symmetric source checks, two-sided cost transmission and the distinction between
classified sell volume and actual downward price response. For conflicts, examine
direct/fiscal/financing costs, inflation, substitution, FX, rates, margins,
consumers, capital flows, beneficiaries, horizon and what is already priced.
Neither side is presumed costless or equally harmed. Apparent selling with a
rising price suggests questions about absorption or classification, not a
verified buyer identity, manipulation, short squeeze or directional signal.

Current Owner examples about U.S. housing/XHB, Treasury yields, Federal Reserve
pressure, Chinese unemployment, domestic priorities, demographics and U.S.-China
conflict are `OWNER_SUPPLIED_CURRENT_CLAIM` / `VERIFICATION_REQUIRED`, not
permanent facts. Any trade relying on them needs current authoritative evidence
at decision time. No present level, policy direction, motive or economic outcome
is asserted by this catalog.

The two new before-entry/while-holding prompts remain voluntary
`REFLECTION_ONLY_NOT_DIAGNOSIS`. Selecting one edits a local draft only; it does
not save, assess, resize, authorize or execute. The 17 bias questions and all
existing capital, exit, market-source and GLD/IBIT boundaries remain unchanged.

Architectural direction only: Alpha aims to reason through event → institution
or policy → causal transmission → economic variable → market expectation →
observed cross-asset response → instrument choice → risk/invalidation → decision
→ later review. The current GLD/IBIT workflow is a validation environment, not
Alpha's entire long-term scope. No new functionality follows from this statement.
