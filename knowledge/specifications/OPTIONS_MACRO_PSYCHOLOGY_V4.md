## Existing material enriched

Capital survival, responsibility, patience and confidence emphasize bounded risk,
no qualifying opportunity as a valid WATCH/NO_TRADE outcome, and euphoria as a
reflection topic. Cash provides liquidity and optionality without being risk-free
in every sense or prescribing allocation. Size follows existing capital/risk
policy, contract economics and evidence, never an emotional confidence multiplier.

Size and hedge material distinguish common factor exposure across tickers without
static correlations or a portfolio score. A hedge may reduce sensitivity and buy
decision time conditionally; costs, basis risk, joint losses and independent exits
remain. FOMO, revenge and entry comfort distinguish a missed opportunity from a
realized loss and require a fresh plan when an old entry condition is gone.

Adding retains the inactive at-most-one planned loser-addition candidate and asks
for prospective conditions, quantity, addition/position ceilings, maximum loss
and thesis validity. Thesis/time stops, tactical review and journal material keep
pre-entry exits and distinguish predefined verified exceptions from loss-aversion
stop drift. Balance and wellbeing remain voluntary attention/monitoring reflection,
without health inference, health data collection or lifestyle prescriptions.
