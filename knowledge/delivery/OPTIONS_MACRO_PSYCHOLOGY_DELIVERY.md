The 72-hour rule, last-thirty-second breakout claims, one loser addition and
winner additions remain inactive candidates. Both 30/30 statements are preserved:
the first batch measures remaining life when the thesis weakens; the second says

Unsupported universal claims are explicitly not adopted: everyone can earn 2%
weekly, a universal 60% loss rate, guaranteed principal protection through options,
inevitable extreme winners, and claims that mental health or nationality determines
trading ability. The weekly compounding example is arithmetic only:
`1.02^52 - 1 ≈ 180%`, not a forecast or target.

## How the page works

In **Pause and ask**, open a phase and choose a question. It appends to the
existing optional personal reflection and leaves other draft fields intact.
The previous preview/request ID is invalidated. Nothing is saved until the Owner
uses **Save local note**. The 1,400-character boundary rejects an overlong addition
without truncation. A selected question is not an admission of bias or a completed
answer. The existing twelve evidence fields and assessment semantics are unchanged.

New saved notes copy content revision `OWNER_PSYCHOLOGY_20260917_V1`, including
questions, sources and corrections. The V1 note contract stays compatible; old
notes recover and retry against their original copied catalog without augmentation
or changed fingerprints. No production notes were manufactured during verification.

Two supplied images are copied and hash-verified privately under
`data/runtime/options-macro-playbook/sources/owner-psychology-20260917`. Their
manifest records the actual September 17 New York receipt, not a claimed creation
date. Images and personal notes are Git-ignored and are not public market evidence.
