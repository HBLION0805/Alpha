# Macro psychology and Owner experience V2 — September 23, 2026

This revision incorporates the Owner's September 23 psychology, macro-risk and
options-management experience into the existing Macro playbook. It is knowledge
and voluntary reflection. The Owner's heuristics have not been externally
validated, and they do not become executable trading rules.

Current content revision: `OWNER_PSYCHOLOGY_20260923_V2`. The original 30 macro
entries remain; the psychology catalog has 22 entries after two additions, for
52 unique items overall. The 17 bias questions are unchanged. Three optional
prompts bring the phase-prompt count to 12. `activeRuleCount` remains zero and
`executionAllowed` remains false.

Existing material on thesis stops, additions, hedges, horizons, options choice,
responsibility, confidence and metaphors now distinguishes an adverse price move
from verified change in the original macro variable; tests a losing-position
addition as a fresh exposure decision; asks what risk a hedge actually offsets;
and checks size after a winning streak against the capital policy. It also makes
option time, realistic strike/price, IV, costs and expiry relevant for both calls
and puts. Independent exits and the original allocation policy still govern.
The one-loss-add limit and a preference to reduce size after wins remain
unapproved process ideas. An 80% decline or 35% rebound is an illustration, not
a threshold. Rolling closes the old position and opens a new one; a debit spread
is a new payoff structure.

`psych-tunnel-vision` is a process guide to read the attractive entry signal
together with counterevidence, invalidation, size, hedge, required data and
reasons to wait or reduce. `psych-short-weakness` is an untested hypothesis:
a large prior gain alone does not justify a short. It adds no stock scanner,
shorting rule, hedge claim or trading universe. Military metaphors remain
illustrations only.

The new prompts are `entry-size-confidence` before entry and
`holding-thesis-change` and `holding-fresh-cash` while holding. They append only
to an explicit local reflection draft; selection does not diagnose a bias,
alter assessment or allocation, or save a note. V1 saved notes continue to
recover from their copied historical catalogs. New notes copy the V2 catalog.
No runtime note was created as part of this revision.
