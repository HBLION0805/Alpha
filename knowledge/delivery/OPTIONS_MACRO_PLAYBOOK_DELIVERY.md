## Corrections retained with the ideas

- Price, thesis and time exits remain separate. A plausible thesis does not cancel
  other pre-existing limits; 30/30 cannot grant a grace period after invalidation.
- -7%, 30/30, profit at 30–50% or 80–100%, 90/10, 50/25/25, and one loss addition
  remain inactive candidates. No recommendation to change current settings.
- Stop prices do not guarantee fills. Spread terminal payoffs require intact
  matching legs and separate costs/exercise handling; long options already limit
  premium loss. A spread is not a frictionless mechanical stop.
- Whole contracts cannot be split. Exact doubling and selling half recovers gross
  premium only under the corresponding execution assumptions; fees and remaining
  market value matter. No rounding or increasing allocation to fit a runner.
- PnL drawings cannot distinguish a future trap from a recovery. They do not
  establish a win rate, price target, dealer intent or the Owner's psychology.
- Open/close timing, quiet weekdays and lifestyle habits are not universal trading
  advantages. Being away requires a monitoring contingency for the chosen horizon.
- Course claims that a price exit must also show thesis damage, or that a good
  process proves a loss was bad luck, are not made universal system rules.

## Local decision notes

The worksheet records horizons, sources/clocks, expectations, transmission and
alternatives, ETF evidence, contract/IV fit, combined exposure, invalidation, time
exits, monitoring, costs/integer exits and counterevidence. Text presence is the
only automated coverage measure; even twelve filled fields do not qualify a trade.

Pre-trade and review notes are explicit owner labels. Server receipt time is
preserved, but timing relative to entry is not automatically verified. Process
adherence and outcome remain independent self-reports. No original-plan reference
or review explanation means the process assessment stays unknown. There is no
automated narrative interpretation, psychological diagnosis or PnL reconciliation.

Explicit save uses the existing protected local API and exclusive fingerprinted
files, copying the catalog and assessment into each note. Exact retries recover
the same record; changed retry content rejects. A revision gets a new ID and can
reference its predecessor. Recovery checks the copied input/catalog; corrupt
records are shown separately without hiding other notes. No production example
or review has been manufactured. Actual execution still belongs in Trade journal.
