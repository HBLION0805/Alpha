# Phase prompts (before entry / while holding / after a decision)

_Verbatim from `knowledge/catalog.json` (OPTIONS_MACRO_PLAYBOOK_V1, revision OWNER_PSYCHOLOGY_20260928_V6, reviewed 2026-09-28). Generated by scripts/render-knowledge.ts — edit the JSON, not this file._

## Before entry

- **Recovery or opportunity?** (`entry-urge`) — Am I trying to recover a loss or catch a missed move? What fresh evidence would support this entry if the previous trade had not happened?
- **Whose conviction?** (`entry-independent`) — If I held no position and had not seen another trader's call, would I choose this exposure? What independent evidence and contrary evidence support my decision?
- **Dollars before upside** (`entry-risk`) — What can the combined position lose under the stated assumptions, including costs and expiry? What is unknown, and can the monitoring plan actually support it?
- **Risk budget or confidence?** (`entry-size-confidence`) — Is the proposed size supported by the existing risk budget, defined loss, costs and evidence, or by a recent winning streak? Could one ordinary loss erase several prior gains?
- **Evidence or allegiance?** (`entry-evidence-or-allegiance`) — Would I interpret the same evidence the same way if the country, government, party or public figure were different? Which part is observed evidence, and which part is political or narrative preference?
- **How many positions are really the same bet?** (`entry-total-exposure`) — If the main market factor moves against this idea, which current positions may lose at the same time? Different tickers need not mean independent risks; what exposure evidence is still missing?
- **Opportunity or another win?** (`entry-euphoria-gate`) — Is this a genuinely qualified new setup, or am I continuing because recent profits make me want another trade or loosen standards? Recent wins do not authorize more size, weaker evidence or looser stops.

## While holding

- **Price anchor or changed evidence?** (`holding-anchor`) — Am I waiting only to get back to my entry price? Which original exit conditions are met, and what evidence has actually changed?
- **Adding, rolling or flipping?** (`holding-change`) — What new evidence justifies this change, what old result will be realized, and what total exposure and costs follow? Closing one direction does not confirm the opposite.
- **Attention and deadline** (`holding-pressure`) — Is pressure or fatigue affecting this decision? What is the original time exit, and what happens if I cannot monitor? No personal health details are needed.
- **Price move or thesis break?** (`holding-thesis-change`) — Is this only an adverse price move, or has a core variable of the original thesis changed? Which dated evidence shows that, and which independent exit conditions are already triggered?
- **Would I buy it fresh today?** (`holding-fresh-cash`) — With no current position and fresh cash, would I buy this exact exposure, strike and expiry today after checking remaining time, premium, total exposure and costs? If not, why consider adding, and what original rule governs holding or exit?
- **Pressure or actual weakness?** (`holding-pressure-or-weakness`) — Is the market showing only apparent selling pressure, or has price confirmed weakness? What distinguishes a volume classification from effective downward price impact?
- **New evidence or moving the goalpost?** (`holding-stop-drift`) — Am I changing the stop because verified information meets a predefined exceptional event, or because realizing the planned loss feels uncomfortable? The original independent exits remain authoritative.
- **Risk management or rescue attempt?** (`holding-rescue-complexity`) — Does this proposed hedge/roll/spread/addition have an independent purpose, incremental risk, cost, total exposure and exit, or am I adding complexity to avoid closing the original trade?
- **Is the thesis valid but this position wrong?** (`holding-thesis-position`) — Could the broader view remain plausible while this strike, expiry, entry, size or structure is no longer appropriate? Which original exits apply, and would any new expression require a fresh plan?
- **Exception or rationalization?** (`holding-rule-exception`) — Which exact rule am I proposing to break, what new evidence makes this situation materially different from the rule's intended case, what extra risk does the exception add, and when does the exception end?

## After a decision

- **Process apart from PnL** (`review-process`) — Against the original dated plan, what did I follow or deviate from? Record the financial result separately; a profit does not prove good process and a loss does not prove poor process.
- **Evidence before blame** (`review-cause`) — Which decisions were controllable, which external problems are documented, and what remains unknown? What specific improvement could be tested prospectively?
- **Review waiting too** (`review-no-trade`) — What evidence supported waiting or rejecting this idea at the time? Do not rewrite that decision because the asset later rose or fell.
- **What actually caused this loss?** (`review-loss-attribution`) — Does the dated plan and execution evidence support prediction, execution, entry timing, sizing/risk, instrument mismatch, missed risk updates, incomplete hedge, plan deviation or normal variance? What remains unknown? Do not force a category from the loss alone.

## Adoption status

- **USE_AS_REFLECTION — Use as questions:** Counterevidence, independent judgment, original-plan comparison, recovery pressure, ticker attachment, costs, monitoring and separate process/outcome review.
- **CANDIDATE_UNTESTED — Keep as inactive proposals:** 72 hours, both conflicting 30/30 versions, one loser addition, adding winners, breakout/retest timing, final-30-second claims, 0DTE one-and-done, an unspecified daily loss limit, two hesitations, realized-profit redeployment and Rule 650 exception discipline. None is an active rule. EUPHORIA_GATE is reflection only.
- **NOT_ADOPTED_AS_FACT — Do not adopt as facts or targets:** Universal weekly returns, unsupported win rates, guaranteed options protection, personality or cultural judgments, and unverified manipulation accusations.
