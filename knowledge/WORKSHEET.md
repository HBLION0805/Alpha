# Trade worksheet fields

_Verbatim from `knowledge/catalog.json` (OPTIONS_MACRO_PLAYBOOK_V1, revision OWNER_PSYCHOLOGY_20260928_V6, reviewed 2026-09-28). Generated by scripts/render-knowledge.ts — edit the JSON, not this file._

1. **Horizon and holding period** (`horizons`) — Separate macro horizon, ETF signal timeframe, intended holding period and option expiry.
2. **Sources and clocks** (`sources`) — Source IDs/links, observation time, receipt time, revisions and known data gaps.
3. **Expectations and surprise** (`expectations`) — What is documented consensus, what is your view, and what is still unknown?
4. **Causal chain and alternatives** (`transmission`) — How does the event reach GLD or IBIT? What else could explain the move?
5. **ETF entry evidence** (`confirmation`) — Specify the traded ETF trigger and available bars; do not substitute an option curve or spot/futures price.
6. **Contract and volatility fit** (`contract`) — Identity, expiry, strike, every leg, IV/Greeks clocks, liquidity and why this structure fits the horizon.
7. **Combined exposure and hedge** (`exposure`) — Total cost and worst-case assumptions across positions; which shared factor could cause losses together, and which risk does a proposed hedge offset?
8. **Thesis and price exits** (`invalidation`) — Before entry: where/how to exit if wrong, what observable evidence invalidates the thesis and which independent price/exposure trigger applies; state precedence.
9. **Catalyst and time exit** (`deadline`) — Before entry: catalyst date/time/zone, review deadline and option exit deadline; avoid indefinite extensions.
10. **Overnight and monitoring contingency** (`monitoring`) — What happens during gaps, unavailable quotes or time away? Who can act and when?
11. **Costs and whole-contract exits** (`costs`) — Before entry: planned profit-taking/target where supported, fees known/unknown, bid/ask, slippage, integer quantities and gross versus net target basis.
12. **Counterevidence and no-trade condition** (`counterevidence`) — What would change your mind? What missing evidence is enough to wait rather than enter?
