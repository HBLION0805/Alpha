## Macro reasoning

### Broaden the evidence base

- **id:** `research` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** context
- **topics:** Travel; Reading; History; English
- **sources:** owner, course

**Principle.** Use diverse viewpoints, original language sources and historical comparisons to challenge a preferred explanation.

**Application.** Record source dates and translation uncertainty; compare the historical regime with the current GLD/IBIT context.

**Limits.** Travel anecdotes are selective observations. Historical resemblance and language fluency do not establish predictive power. These are personal research practices, not return factors.

### From a macro story to a testable hypothesis

- **id:** `expectations` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** context
- **topics:** Economics; Policy transmission; Consensus; Scenario analysis
- **sources:** owner, course, thesis

**Principle.** Describe a causal chain, competing outcomes and the difference between your view and documented market expectations.

**Application.** Separate event expectations, verified actuals, rates/USD transmission and observed GLD/IBIT prices. Label subjective probabilities; leave unsupported probabilities unknown.

**Limits.** Neither a correct policy forecast nor a made-up scenario probability establishes an option opportunity. Correlations and policy reactions vary by regime.

### Direction needs a horizon

- **id:** `horizons` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** guidance
- **topics:** Scalper; Day trader; Swing trader; Position trader; Long-term investor; Investor research cadence; Trader research cadence
- **sources:** owner, owner-psychology-20260923

**Principle.** Keep the macro thesis horizon, ETF signal timeframe, intended holding period and option expiration separate. Investors may research a business or macro thesis deeply up front and reassess periodically; traders need more frequent relevant updates, including overnight changes.

**Application.** Explain how a five-minute setup fits the intended holding window and why the contract has sufficient time for the thesis. Before acting as a trader, refresh the thesis, risk and position using source-dated evidence; an investor need not react to every intraday move.

**Limits.** Neither style is inherently superior. Long-term conviction cannot justify holding an expiring option indefinitely, and constant monitoring cannot turn stale or weak sources into certainty. The five trader types are approximate; current collection cadence does not support reliable scalping.

### Style is a research lens

- **id:** `styles` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** guidance
- **topics:** Value; Contrarian; Catalyst / speculative; Fundamental / growth; Macro; Narrative
- **sources:** owner

**Principle.** Identify whether the idea rests on valuation, disputed consensus, a catalyst, business growth, macro transmission or changing beliefs.

**Application.** For GLD/IBIT, explain the underlying exposure and ETF link; do not import operating-company valuation assumptions without a model.

**Limits.** Styles overlap. Contrarian does not mean automatically opposing consensus; a compelling narrative or catalyst does not establish a favorable price or probability.

### Mechanisms need evidence

- **id:** `mechanisms` · **status:** MECHANICS_REFERENCE · **destination:** guidance
- **topics:** Dealer hedging; Liquidity; Positioning; Open interest; Delta; Gamma; Theta; Vega
- **sources:** owner, greeks

**Principle.** Greeks describe model sensitivities; liquidity and positioning can affect how a macro view is expressed.

**Application.** Use the saved sensitivities with their clocks, bid/ask and contract identity. Distinguish a possible mechanism from an observed one.

**Limits.** Open interest and volume alone do not identify dealer direction. Delta is not a profit peak or a verified win probability. No dealer-flow detector is introduced.

## Decision process

### Update when evidence changes

- **id:** `uncertainty` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** reviews
- **topics:** Uncertainty; Imperfection; Mistakes
- **sources:** owner, review

**Principle.** Keep an alternative explanation and a clear observation that would contradict the thesis.

**Application.** Record what changed and which evidence was available at the decision time.

**Limits.** Loss alone does not prove a bad decision; profit does not prove skill. Do not automatically attribute a loss to luck, fear, manipulation or a broker.

### Waiting is a valid decision

- **id:** `patience` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** guidance
- **topics:** Boredom; No trade; Selectivity; Cash; Optionality
- **sources:** owner, course, owner-psychology-20260926, mentor-discipline

**Principle.** Document missing evidence and the observation that would justify another review. Choosing not to trade is a valid option with unclear direction, poor risk/reward, low liquidity, noisy structure, missing evidence or voluntarily reported insufficient attention/readiness.

**Application.** Keep WATCH, NO_TRADE or cash when no opportunity qualifies. Cash can preserve low market exposure, liquidity, buying power, optionality and the ability to wait; capital need not be forced into a position. Canonical text: knowledge/mentor/discipline/discipline-01-20.md 纪律 #02, 纪律 #12. Encoded in src/options/discipline.ts sessionGate (market-not-worth-trading → NO_TRADE); CASH_NOTE in digest + macro CHECKLIST.

**Limits.** Fewer trades alone do not improve expectancy. Cash is not risk-free in every sense; there is no allocation target or automatic capital movement. Readiness is voluntary reflection, never inferred health or an automatic psychological gate. Patience does not cancel a defined exit or monitoring obligation.

### Make the monitoring plan sustainable

- **id:** `balance` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** planner
- **topics:** Leisure; Sleep and rest; Exercise; Meals; Meditation; Work/life balance; Microscopic trading; Monitoring capacity
- **sources:** owner, owner-psychology-20260926, mentor-discipline

**Principle.** Choose a review cadence compatible with the strategy and record who can monitor the position.

**Application.** Include a contingency for being away, a stopped collector or unavailable quotes before relying on a short-lived option setup. Voluntarily consider attention, monitoring capacity and time away; constant watching when planned work is complete, no opportunity exists or markets are closed is not automatically productive. **Limits.** No lifestyle behavior is prescribed and no health or relationship information is collected or inferred. Lifestyle examples are not a diagnosis or proven edge. Quiet Mondays, midday breaks, open-for-exits and close-for-entries are not universal rules; existing exit obligations remain.

### Process adherence and PnL are separate

- **id:** `quality` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** reviews
- **topics:** Confidence formula; Outcome bias; Good process / loss; Poor process / profit
- **sources:** owner, review

**Principle.** Review process against the original plan and then separately record the financial outcome.

**Application.** Keep both assessments unknown when supporting evidence is absent; inspect assumptions even when execution followed the plan.

**Limits.** Successes divided by failures is an illustrative confidence metaphor, not a calibrated statistic. Adherence to an unprofitable strategy does not validate it. Do not automatically label outcomes deserved or lucky.

### Distinguish challenged and invalidated

- **id:** `thesis-stop` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** journal
- **topics:** Thesis stop; Do not rewrite the reason; PRICE_ADVERSE_BUT_THESIS_NOT_INVALIDATED; THESIS_CORE_VARIABLE_CHANGED; Capital survival; LOSS_AVERSION_STOP_DRIFT
- **sources:** owner, thesis, owner-psychology-20260923, owner-psychology-20260926, mentor-stop-loss, mentor-discipline

**Principle.** PRICE_ADVERSE_BUT_THESIS_NOT_INVALIDATED names an adverse price move without verified invalidation; loss alone does not prove the macro thesis wrong. THESIS_CORE_VARIABLE_CHANGED names a verified change in an original core variable that requires reassessment.

**Application.** Before entry identify the relevant war, sanctions, energy, central-bank policy, rates, dollar, liquidity or other original macro variable and its evidence source. Document independent exits for wrong/right outcomes and thesis invalidation before entry. Compare new facts with the saved thesis, then apply price, time, capital and contract exits; preserving capital protects future participation. Canonical text: knowledge/mentor/discipline/stop-loss.md §Thesis Stop Loss. Encoded in src/options/stops.ts thesisStop + pretrade thesisRecord. Canonical text: knowledge/mentor/discipline/discipline-01-20.md 纪律 #17, 纪律 #18.

**Limits.** These terms are educational, not an automatic state machine. A compelling story cannot override an independent exit. Moving a stop farther away solely to avoid realizing a loss is LOSS_AVERSION_STOP_DRIFT; the plan remains frozen after entry except already predefined exceptions supported by new verified information. No new stop percentage. Neither hedge nor averaging repairs a broken thesis or erases a loss; requiring both price and thesis damage is not a universal rule.

### Defined payoff is not a guaranteed exit

- **id:** `spread-risk` · **status:** MECHANICS_REFERENCE · **destination:** planner
- **topics:** Built-in stop; Call debit spread; Put debit spread; Credit spreads; Iron condor
- **sources:** owner, spread, mentor-stop-loss

**Principle.** For a standard intact equal-quantity, same-expiry bull call spread, gross terminal loss is the net debit; maximum gross gain is strike width less debit.

**Application.** Include the multiplier, all legs and costs. Call terminal breakeven is long strike plus debit; describe exercise and assignment handling separately. Canonical text: knowledge/mentor/discipline/stop-loss.md §Built-In Stop Loss. Encoded in src/options/stops.ts multiLegChecklist.

**Limits.** Operational exposure can change through legging, assignment or expiration. Long calls/puts already limit premium loss before exercise. Other structures need their own payoff models; this catalog does not enable them.

## Psychology and evidence

### Entry comfort is not entry evidence

- **id:** `psych-entry-comfort` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** journal
- **topics:** $11 / $13 / $12 example; Breakeven fixation; Entry discomfort
- **sources:** owner-psychology, owner-psychology-20260926

**Principle.** Separate the discomfort of an early loss from evidence that an entry has become invalid.

**Application.** Before entry, record a prospective ETF confirmation and what would contradict it. During review, compare actions with that plan rather than the purchase price alone. The same direction at a materially different entry price can be a different risk/reward trade; if the original entry condition has gone, require a fresh setup.

**Limits.** The later rise in the example is hindsight. Buying a pullback at $12 is not inherently safer than $11; a higher price or immediate profit does not prove confirmation. No automatic price-too-high threshold.

### Do not ask the next trade to repay the last

- **id:** `psych-revenge` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** planner
- **topics:** Revenge trading; FOMO; Recovery pressure; Missed opportunity
- **sources:** owner-psychology, owner-psychology-20260926, mentor-discipline

**Principle.** Treat each proposed entry as a fresh decision with its own evidence, costs and exposure.

**Application.** Ask whether the same idea would be considered without the recent loss, missed rally or someone else's reported profit. Missing a trade is not a realized financial loss; the next trade needs independent justification, not a claim that the market owes the lost money. Voluntarily review faster entries, larger size, lower standards, looser stops or entries without a qualified setup. Canonical text: knowledge/mentor/discipline/discipline-01-20.md 纪律 #15. Encoded in src/options/discipline.ts sizeDisciplineCheck.

**Limits.** A loss is still a financial loss when described as a strategic retreat. Prior profit is not free risk capital. Self-reported warning signs are reflection, not inferred diagnosis; no automatic cooldown or emotion-triggered trade ban is introduced.

### Confidence needs calibration, not a sizing multiplier

- **id:** `psych-confidence` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** reviews
- **topics:** Confidence formula; High conviction; Guru confidence; Hot streak; Tenfold opportunity
- **sources:** owner-psychology, owner-psychology-20260923, owner-psychology-20260926, mentor-discipline

**Principle.** Keep personal conviction, process adherence and independently measured results separate. Recent wins do not authorize a larger position.

**Application.** Record evidence and alternatives before relying on a trader, AI or a winning streak. EUPHORIA_GATE is a reflection question: qualified new opportunity, desire for another win, or willingness to loosen standards? Ask whether one ordinary loss could erase several prior gains; size remains based on capital policy, defined maximum loss, instrument economics, total account exposure, evidence quality and current risk constraints. Canonical text: knowledge/mentor/discipline/discipline-01-20.md 纪律 #03, 纪律 #09. Encoded in src/options/discipline.ts sizeDisciplineCheck.

**Limits.** Successes/failures is not a calibrated probability; zero failures does not imply infinite skill. Fewer trades need not improve expectancy. Excitement, anger, revenge, recent wins and hot-hand feelings never authorize a sizing multiplier, lower evidence standards, looser stops or extra trades. EUPHORIA_GATE is not an active execution gate; no automatic size change.

### Closing one direction does not establish the opposite

- **id:** `psych-direction` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** guidance
- **topics:** Direction switch; No longer bearish; Neutral; Options sideways decay
- **sources:** owner-psychology

**Principle.** An exit and an opposite entry answer different questions.

**Application.** State independent evidence for a new direction, contract fit and exits. Include remaining positions if both directions would coexist.

**Limits.** A price that stops falling has not necessarily begun an uptrend. Neutral is valid; opposing option premiums can both lose value.

### Observe again without rewriting the original plan

- **id:** `psych-tactical` · **status:** PROCESS_GUIDE · **destination:** guidance
- **topics:** Tactical awareness; Regime change; Uncertainty; Chart limitations
- **sources:** owner-psychology, owner-psychology-20260923, owner-psychology-20260926

**Principle.** Compare current evidence with the original expectations, source clocks and predefined exits. Distinguish adverse price movement from a change in the thesis core variables.
